Drink Your Water — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Applies to: the Drink Your Water apps for Android and iPhone (listed in the stores as "Drink Your Water: Check-ins").

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • We collect nothing. Your reminders, habits and check-in history stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • No tracking, ads or analytics. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it doesn't connect to the internet.
  • You're in control. Delete anything at any time in the app, or uninstall to remove it all.

We built Drink Your Water this way on purpose. We don't think a habit app should know more about you than it needs to, and this one doesn't need to know anything.

Who we are

Drink Your Water is made by Jarrod Simpson ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@jarrodqsimpson.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your reminders and habits: names, times, repeat settings, active days, and any custom habit frameworks and messages you create.
  • Your check-in history: when you confirmed a check-in and how many times you snoozed it. This powers your streaks and progress screen.
  • Your settings: color theme, motivational style, notification chime, vacation mode, Strict Mode (Android), and whether you've finished onboarding.

This information lives in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. The Android app doesn't request internet access at all, and neither app sends data over the network.

Permissions the app uses

Permission Why
Notifications To deliver your reminders, and to re-alert you if a check-in goes unanswered.
Exact alarms (Android) So reminders arrive at the time you set rather than whenever Android gets around to it.
Run after restart (Android) To reschedule your reminders after your phone restarts.
Vibration (Android) So reminders can vibrate.
Time Sensitive notifications and Background App Refresh (iPhone) So reminders can come through Focus modes, and so the app can keep your upcoming reminders scheduled. Background refresh only schedules reminders on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
Screen pinning (Android, optional) Used only if you turn on Strict Mode. It keeps the check-in screen in front until you confirm or snooze. It's a standard Android feature you control, and Android always keeps an exit gesture available.

You can turn notifications off, or revoke any permission, in your phone's settings. Reminders won't arrive without notifications.

Backups made by your phone

Android and iPhone can include app data in the backups they make to your Google or Apple account (for example Android Backup or iCloud Backup), depending on your settings. Those backups are made and stored by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We can't access them. You can turn backups off in your phone's settings.

App stores

You download the app from Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google and Apple handle downloads, and they may collect information under their own privacy policies. If your phone shares diagnostics with app developers (a setting you control), Google or Apple may show us anonymous crash and performance reports. These include things like device model, OS version and where the app crashed. They don't include your reminders or check-in history.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or receive your personal information.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data with anyone, because we don't have any.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't show ads.

Deleting your data

  • Edit or delete any reminder or habit in the app.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete All Data (on Android, "Delete All Account Data"). This erases your reminders, habits, history and streaks and restarts the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone. Copies may remain in your phone's own backups until you delete those.

Children

Drink Your Water isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, California and similar laws)

These laws give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you, such as access, correction, deletion and opting out of sale. Because we don't collect or hold any personal data, there's nothing for us to access, correct, delete or sell. Your data stays on your device, where you can view and delete it yourself. If you have questions, contact us at support@jarrodqsimpson.com.

Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security: app sandboxing, your lock screen, and device encryption. Keeping your phone locked and up to date is the best way to protect it.

How this app was made (AI disclosure)

We built Drink Your Water with the help of AI software development tools, including Anthropic's Claude and Google AI Studio. We reviewed and tested what they produced. The app itself has no AI features: nothing you type or do in the app is sent to any AI service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and post the new version at https://jarrodqsimpson.com/drink-your-water/privacy. If a change affects how your data is handled, for example if we ever add a feature that sends data off your device, we'll explain it in the app before it takes effect.
