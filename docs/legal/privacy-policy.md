# Drink Your Water — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

**Applies to:** the Drink Your Water apps for Android and iPhone (listed in the stores as "Drink Your Water: Check-ins").

## The short version

- **Android: no account.** The Android app never asks you to sign up or sign in. Your reminders, habits and check-in history stay on your phone, and we never receive them.
- **iPhone: an account.** The iPhone app uses an account (email and password, or Google sign-in), and keeps your reminders, habits, settings and check-in history on our service provider's servers so they're tied to your account.
- **Subscriptions.** If you buy a subscription, the purchase is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous ID and your purchase status so the app can unlock what you bought.
- **No tracking, ads or analytics.** Neither app contains advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and we never sell your data.
- **You're in control.** Delete your data at any time in the app, or ask us to delete your account.

We built Drink Your Water to know as little about you as it can. This policy explains exactly what is stored, where, and why.

## Who we are

Drink Your Water is made by Jarrod Simpson ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@jarrodqsimpson.com.

## What the app stores, and where

The apps keep the following:

- **Your reminders and habits:** names, times, repeat settings, active days, and any custom habit frameworks and messages you create.
- **Your check-in history:** when you confirmed a check-in and how many times you snoozed it. This powers your streaks and progress screen.
- **Your settings:** color theme, motivational style, notification chime, vacation mode, Strict Mode (Android), and whether you've finished onboarding.

**Android.** This information lives **on your device only**, in the app's private storage. Other apps can't read it, and the app never sends it to us or anyone else.

**iPhone.** This information is stored with your account on servers run by Base44, the platform that hosts the iPhone app's backend. We receive and can access it through that platform. It is used only to run the app for you. Your account itself consists of your email address (or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google) and your sign-in credentials, which Base44 handles.

## Subscriptions and purchases

The apps may offer optional paid subscriptions that unlock additional reminder types. The water reminder itself is free.

- **Payments** are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment card or billing details.
- **RevenueCat** is the service we use to confirm what you've purchased. To do that, the app sends it an anonymous app user ID (random, not your name or email) and the purchase and subscription status that Google or Apple reports, along with basic technical information such as your app version and device type. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf, and only to unlock and restore what you bought. It isn't used for advertising, and we don't sell it. RevenueCat's own policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
- **Internet access.** The Android app uses internet access only for this purpose. If you never subscribe, nothing about your reminders, habits or check-ins is sent anywhere.

## Permissions the app uses

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To deliver your reminders, and to re-alert you if a check-in goes unanswered. |
| Exact alarms (Android) | So reminders arrive at the time you set rather than whenever Android gets around to it. |
| Run after restart (Android) | To reschedule your reminders after your phone restarts. |
| Internet and network state (Android) | To check subscription status with Google Play and RevenueCat. Your reminders and check-in history are not sent. |
| Billing (Android) | To let you buy or restore a subscription through Google Play. |
| Vibration (Android) | So reminders can vibrate. |
| Time Sensitive notifications and Background App Refresh (iPhone) | So reminders can come through Focus modes, and so the app can keep your upcoming reminders scheduled. Background refresh only schedules reminders on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. |
| Screen pinning (Android, optional) | Used only if you turn on Strict Mode. It keeps the check-in screen in front until you confirm or snooze. It's a standard Android feature you control, and Android always keeps an exit gesture available. |

You can turn notifications off, or revoke any permission, in your phone's settings. Reminders won't arrive without notifications.

## Backups made by your phone

Android and iPhone can include the app's local data in the backups they make to your Google or Apple account (for example Android Backup or iCloud Backup), depending on your settings. Those backups are made and stored by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We can't access them. You can turn backups off in your phone's settings.

## App stores

You download the app from Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google and Apple handle downloads, and they may collect information under their own privacy policies. If your phone shares diagnostics with app developers (a setting you control), Google or Apple may show us anonymous crash and performance reports. These include things like device model, OS version and where the app crashed. They don't include your reminders or check-in history.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or rent your data.
- We don't share it with anyone except the service providers described above (Base44 for the iPhone app's backend, and RevenueCat for subscriptions), who process it only on our behalf.
- We don't track you across apps or websites.
- We don't show ads, and the apps contain no advertising or analytics code.
- On Android, we don't collect your reminders, habits or check-in history at all.

## Deleting your data

- **Edit or delete** any reminder or habit in the app.
- **Android, delete everything:** Settings → Delete All Account Data. This erases your reminders, habits, history and streaks on your phone and restarts the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data. Copies may remain in your phone's own backups until you delete those.
- **iPhone, delete your data:** Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your reminders, habits, settings and check-in history from our servers and signs you out. To have your sign-in account itself (your email address or Google sign-in link) removed as well, email support@jarrodqsimpson.com from the address on the account and we'll delete it.
- **Subscription records.** Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple and by RevenueCat. To ask RevenueCat to delete your anonymous purchase record, email us at support@jarrodqsimpson.com and we'll submit the request. Cancel a subscription in Google Play or your Apple ID settings; deleting the app or your data does not cancel it.

## Children

Drink Your Water isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or made a purchase, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, California and similar laws)

These laws give you rights over the personal data a company holds about you, such as access, correction, deletion and opting out of sale. We don't sell personal data. On Android, your reminders and history stay on your device, where you can view and delete them yourself. For an iPhone account, or for a subscription record, you can ask us to access, correct or delete what we hold by emailing support@jarrodqsimpson.com, and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

## Security

Data on your phone is protected by your phone's own security: app sandboxing, your lock screen, and device encryption. Data sent to Base44 or RevenueCat is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure, so keeping your phone locked and up to date remains the best protection.

## How this app was made (AI disclosure)

We built Drink Your Water with the help of AI software development tools, including Anthropic's Claude and Google AI Studio. We reviewed and tested what they produced. The app itself has **no AI features**: nothing you type or do in the app is sent to any AI service.
